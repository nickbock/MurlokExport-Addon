### 1.7.3
- Updated for patch 12.1.5
- Fixed an error at login, and the Consumables panel missing from the Auction House, when another addon loads the Auction House early

### 1.7.2
- The number next to each hero talent in the class list now shows how many of the spec's top 50 players play that hero talent, as on Murlok.io (for example 47 Lightsmith and 3 Herald of the Sun for Holy Paladin). Gear, talents and stats are still those of that hero talent's own top 50 players.

### 1.7.1
- The Consumables date now says what it is: the date of the Wowhead guide's last change. The recommendations are checked against the guides every week.
- At the Auction House, the button that reopens the Consumables panel is now a side tab on the edge of the Auction House window, like the tabs next to the quest log.

### 1.7.0
- New: Consumables. The recommended flasks, potions, food, weapon buffs, enchants and gems for each spec, from the Wowhead class guides. These are PvE recommendations, so they show with the Mythic+ list: on the Consumables tab in the compact view, or behind the Consumables button in the Equipment panel when maximized.
- New: at the Auction House, a Consumables panel lists what your current spec should buy, with how many you already have. Click an item to search for it.
- The recommendations come from Gorloc Consumables, a separate free addon: https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/gorloc-consumables
- A short "What's new" window now shows once after an update that brings something new.
